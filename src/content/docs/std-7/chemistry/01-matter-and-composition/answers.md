## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: State two main postulates of kinetic theory.
   - **Answer**:
     - Matter consists of tiny particles in continuous random motion. [1 Mark]
     - Particles possess intermolecular forces of attraction and intermolecular spaces between them. [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: Evaporation vs Boiling:
   - Evaporation is a surface phenomenon occurring at all temperatures; Boiling is a bulk phenomenon occurring at a fixed boiling point. [1 Mark]
   - Evaporation produces a cooling effect; Boiling does not. [1 Mark]
3. **Question**: Why do solids have definite shape and volume?
   - **Answer**: Intermolecular forces are very strong and intermolecular spaces are negligible, locking particles into fixed positions. [2 Marks]
4. **Question**: Sublimation and examples:
   - **Answer**: Sublimation is the process where a solid changes directly into gas on heating without passing through liquid state. [1 Mark] Examples: Camphor, Naphthalene, Iodine. [1 Mark]
5. **Question**: Why are gases easily compressible?
   - **Answer**: Gases have very large intermolecular spaces between widely separated particles, which can be easily decreased by applying external pressure. [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Change of state:
   - (a) Latent heat of fusion: Heat energy required to convert 1 kg of solid into liquid at its melting point without any rise in temperature. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Earthen pot cooling: Water seeps through porous walls and evaporates from the outer surface. Evaporation draws latent heat from the remaining water, cooling it. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Boiling point: Increasing pressure increases the boiling point of a liquid (principle of pressure cooker). [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: Diffusion & kinetic theory:
   - (a) Diffusion is the spontaneous intermingling of particles of two or more substances on their own. Example: Aroma of perfume spreading across a room. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Higher temperature increases the kinetic energy and speed of particles, causing them to move and mix faster. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Cohesive force: Attraction between like molecules. Adhesive force: Attraction between unlike molecules. [1 Mark]
