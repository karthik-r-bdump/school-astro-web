## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. Which of the following substances undergoes sublimation?
   - A) Sodium chloride
   - B) Ammonium chloride
   - C) Calcium carbonate
   - D) Copper sulphate

2. In which state of matter is the intermolecular force of attraction strongest?
   - A) Liquid
   - B) Gas
   - C) Solid
   - D) Plasma

3. When temperature increases, the kinetic energy of particles:
   - A) Decreases
   - B) Increases
   - C) Remains constant
   - D) First increases then decreases

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)

   - (b) Explain why water stored in an earthen pot (matka) cools during summer. [2 Marks]
   - (c) What is the effect of increasing pressure on the boiling point of a liquid? [1 Mark]

   - (a) What is diffusion? Give one real-life example in gases. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Why does diffusion occur faster at higher temperatures? [2 Marks]
   - (c) Define cohesive and adhesive forces. [1 Mark]
