# Chapter 1: Matter and Its Composition

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Definition of matter: Anything that occupies space and has mass.
- Kinetic Molecular Theory of Matter (Postulates: composition of particles, intermolecular space, intermolecular force of attraction, continuous random motion).
- Three states of matter: Solids, Liquids, Gases.
- Inter-conversion of states: Melting, Vaporization, Condensation, Freezing, Sublimation, Deposition.
- Evaporation vs Boiling.

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Kinetic Molecular Theory of Matter
1. Matter is made up of tiny particles called **atoms** or **molecules**.
2. Particles have spaces between them called **intermolecular space**.
3. Particles attract one another with forces called **intermolecular forces of attraction** (cohesive force between same molecules; adhesive force between different molecules).
4. Particles possess kinetic energy and are in **continuous random motion**, which increases with temperature.

### 2. Comparison of the Three States of Matter
| Property | Solid | Liquid | Gas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shape & Volume** | Definite shape and volume | Indefinite shape, definite volume | Indefinite shape and volume |
| **Intermolecular Space** | Extremely small / closely packed | Moderate | Very large / widely separated |
| **Intermolecular Force** | Very strong | Moderate | Extremely weak / negligible |
| **Compressibility** | Incompressible | Almost incompressible | Highly compressible |
| **Fluidity & Rigidity** | Rigid, cannot flow | Fluid, flows from higher to lower | Highly fluid, flows in all directions |

### 3. Change of State Phenomena
- **Melting**: Solid $\rightarrow$ Liquid at constant melting point.
- **Vaporization**: Liquid $\rightarrow$ Gas at boiling point.
- **Sublimation**: Direct change from solid to gas without entering liquid state (e.g., Ammonium chloride, Camphor, Iodine, Naphthalene).
- **Deposition**: Direct change from gas to solid.
