<Quiz title="Kinetic Theory of Matter" autoNumber>
According to the Kinetic Molecular Theory of Matter, which state of matter has the maximum intermolecular space and minimum intermolecular force of attraction?

- [ ] Solid state
- [ ] Liquid state
- [x] Gaseous state
- [ ] Plasma state

**Explanation:** In gases, molecules are situated far apart from each other, resulting in very large intermolecular spaces and negligible intermolecular forces of attraction. This allows gas molecules to move freely in all directions.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Sublimating Substances" autoNumber>
Which of the following substances change directly from a solid into a gas without passing through the liquid state upon heating? (Select all that apply)

- [x] Camphor
- [x] Ammonium chloride
- [ ] Common salt (Sodium chloride)
- [x] Iodine
- [ ] Sugar

**Explanation:** Camphor, ammonium chloride, iodine, and dry ice (solid $\text{CO}_2$) sublime when heated. Common salt and sugar do not sublime; they melt or decompose upon heating.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Random Motion of Particles" autoNumber>
The continuous, random, zigzag motion of suspended particles in a liquid or gas is known as [[Brownian]] motion.

**Explanation:** Discovered by botanist Robert Brown in 1827, Brownian motion provides direct visual evidence that molecules in fluids are in constant, continuous, and rapid random motion.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Properties of Liquids" autoNumber>
Which property correctly describes a liquid?

- [ ] Definite shape and definite volume
- [x] Definite volume but no definite shape
- [ ] No definite shape and no definite volume
- [ ] High rigidity and incompressibility

**Explanation:** Liquids have a definite volume because their intermolecular attraction is strong enough to keep molecules together, but no definite shape because molecules can slide past one another to take the shape of the container.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Constant Temperature During Boiling" autoNumber>
The temperature at which a liquid changes into vapor throughout its volume at normal atmospheric pressure is called its [[boiling]] point.

**Explanation:** During boiling, the heat supplied is consumed as latent heat of vaporization to overcome intermolecular forces between molecules, keeping the temperature constant until the entire liquid converts into vapor.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Rigidity and Compressibility" autoNumber>
Why are solids practically incompressible?

- [x] Intermolecular spaces are negligible and molecules are closely packed.
- [ ] Solids have very low densities.
- [ ] Kinetic energy of solid molecules is very high.
- [ ] Intermolecular forces are completely absent.

**Explanation:** In solids, constituent particles are closely packed with strong electrostatic forces and almost negligible intermolecular spaces, making it impossible to push the particles closer together under ordinary pressure.
</Quiz>
