## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Define valency with respect to hydrogen.
   - **Answer**: Valency is the number of hydrogen atoms that can combine with or be displaced by one atom of that element. [2 Marks]
2. **Question**: Chemical formulae:
   - (a) Calcium nitrate: $Ca(NO_3)_2$ [0.5 Mark]
   - (b) Ferric chloride: $FeCl_3$ [0.5 Mark]
   - (c) Ammonium carbonate: $(NH_4)_2CO_3$ [0.5 Mark]
   - (d) Zinc sulphate: $ZnSO_4$ [0.5 Mark]
3. **Question**: Balance $Zn + HCl \rightarrow ZnCl_2 + H_2$:
   - **Answer**: $Zn + 2HCl \rightarrow ZnCl_2 + H_2$ [2 Marks]
4. **Question**: Law of Conservation of Mass:
   - **Answer**: Matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction; total mass of reactants equals total mass of products. [2 Marks]
5. **Question**: Radical definition and example:
   - **Answer**: A group of atoms of different elements that behaves as a single unit with a charge. Example: Sulphate ($SO_4^{2-}$), Carbonate ($CO_3^{2-}$). [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Balanced equations:
   - (a) $2KClO_3 \rightarrow 2KCl + 3O_2$ [2 Marks]
   - (b) $3Fe + 4H_2O \rightarrow Fe_3O_4 + 4H_2$ [2 Marks]
   - (c) $2NaOH + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow Na_2SO_4 + 2H_2O$ [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: Molecular masses & reason:
   - (a) $MgCO_3$: $24 + 12 + (3 \times 16) = 24 + 12 + 48 = 84\,\text{amu}$. [2 Marks]
   - (b) $H_2O$: $(2 \times 1) + 16 = 18\,\text{amu}$. [1 Mark]
   - (c) A chemical equation must be balanced to obey the Law of Conservation of Mass, ensuring the number of atoms of each element is equal on both sides. [2 Marks]
