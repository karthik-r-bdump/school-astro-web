## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. The chemical formula of Aluminum sulphate is:
   - A) $AlSO_4$
   - B) $Al_2(SO_4)_3$
   - C) $Al_3(SO_4)_2$
   - D) $Al_2SO_4$

2. The valency of nitrogen in Ammonia ($NH_3$) is:
   - A) 1
   - B) 2
   - C) 3
   - D) 4

3. Which of the following is a divalent basic radical?
   - A) $Na^+$
   - B) $Al^{3+}$
   - C) $Ca^{2+}$
   - D) $NH_4^+$

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. Define valency with respect to hydrogen.
2. Write the chemical formulae of:
   - (a) Calcium nitrate
   - (b) Ferric chloride
   - (c) Ammonium carbonate
   - (d) Zinc sulphate
3. Balance the following chemical equation:
   $$Zn + HCl \rightarrow ZnCl_2 + H_2$$
4. State the Law of Conservation of Mass in a chemical reaction.
5. What is a radical? Give one example of a polyatomic anion.

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. Balance each of the following chemical equations:
   - (a) $KClO_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} KCl + O_2$ [2 Marks]
   - (b) $Fe + H_2O \rightarrow Fe_3O_4 + H_2$ [2 Marks]
   - (c) $NaOH + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow Na_2SO_4 + H_2O$ [1 Mark]

2. Write the chemical formula and calculate the molecular mass of:
   - (a) Magnesium carbonate ($MgCO_3$), given $Mg=24, C=12, O=16$. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Water ($H_2O$), given $H=1, O=16$. [1 Mark]
   - (c) Explain why a chemical equation must always be balanced. [2 Marks]
