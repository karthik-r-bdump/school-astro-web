## Section II (40 Marks)

### Question 5 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Physical vs Chemical Change** [3 Marks]:
  - Physical: Temporary, reversible, no new substance formed, mass unchanged. [1.5 Marks]
  - Chemical: Permanent, irreversible, new substances with distinct properties formed, energy absorbed/evolved. [1.5 Marks]
- (b) **Candle burning dual nature** [3 Marks]:
  - Melting of wax is a physical change (solid wax melts into liquid wax and resolidifies without chemical change). [1.5 Marks]
  - Burning of the wick and vaporized wax produces $CO_2$, water vapor, heat, and light, which cannot be reversed (chemical change). [1.5 Marks]
- (c) **Atomicity** [4 Marks]:
  - Definition: The number of atoms present in one molecule of an element. [1 Mark]
  - Monoatomic: Helium ($He$) / Neon ($Ne$) [1 Mark]
  - Diatomic: Oxygen ($O_2$) / Nitrogen ($N_2$) [1 Mark]
  - Triatomic: Ozone ($O_3$) [1 Mark]

### Question 6 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Kinetic Theory Postulates** [3 Marks]:
  - Matter consists of molecules in ceaseless random motion. [1 Mark]
  - Molecules possess kinetic energy that increases directly with temperature. [1 Mark]
  - Intermolecular spaces and intermolecular forces of attraction govern physical state. [1 Mark]
- (b) **Mixture Separation** [4 Marks]:
  - 1. Bring a magnet near the mixture to separate **Iron filings** (magnetic attraction). [1 Mark]
  - 2. Heat the remaining mixture in an evaporating dish covered with an inverted funnel plugged with cotton. **Ammonium chloride** sublimes and deposits on cooler funnel walls. [2 Marks]
  - 3. The residue left in the dish is **sand**. [1 Mark]
- (c) **Constant temperature during melting** [3 Marks]:
  - The heat supplied is absorbed as **latent heat of fusion** to overcome the intermolecular forces holding the ice lattice together, without increasing the average kinetic energy of molecules. [3 Marks]

### Question 7 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Element $X$ ($Z=12, A=24$)** [5 Marks]:
  - (i) Protons = 12, Electrons = 12, Neutrons = $24 - 12 = 12$. [3 Marks]
  - (ii) Configuration = $2, 8, 2$. Valency = $+2$ (loses 2 valence electrons). [2 Marks]
- (b) **Law of Conservation of Mass** [3 Marks]:
  - Total mass of silver nitrate + sodium chloride in solution before reaction equals total mass of white precipitate of silver chloride + sodium nitrate solution after reaction. [3 Marks]
- (c) **Radicals** [2 Marks]:
  - A radical is a charged group of bonded atoms behaving as a single unit. [1 Mark]
  - Basic radicals are positively charged cations ($NH_4^+, Cu^{2+}$); Acidic radicals are negatively charged anions ($SO_4^{2-}, NO_3^-$). [1 Mark]
