## Section I (40 Marks) — Compulsory

### Question 1: Multiple Choice Questions [10 Marks]
1. The process of converting a liquid into its vapor below its boiling point is:
   - A) Boiling
   - B) Evaporation
   - C) Sublimation
   - D) Condensation
2. The formula of Calcium phosphate is:
   - A) $CaPO_4$
   - B) $Ca_3(PO_4)_2$
   - C) $Ca_2(PO_4)_3$
   - D) $Ca(PO_4)_2$
3. A mixture of ammonium chloride and sand can be separated by:
   - A) Filtration
   - B) Sublimation
   - C) Evaporation
   - D) Magnetic separation
4. The atomicity of Ozone is:
   - A) 1
   - B) 2
   - C) 3
   - D) 4
5. Which of the following is a chemical change?
   - A) Melting of wax
   - B) Dissolving sugar in water
   - C) Rusting of iron
   - D) Boiling of water
6. The subatomic particle with no charge is:
   - A) Proton
   - B) Electron
   - C) Neutron
   - D) Positron
7. The maximum number of electrons that can be accommodated in the L shell is:
   - A) 2
   - B) 8
   - C) 18
   - D) 32
8. The valency of carbon in Methane ($CH_4$) is:
   - A) 1
   - B) 2
   - C) 3
   - D) 4
9. Which of the following is a compound?
   - A) Air
   - B) Milk
   - C) Pure water
   - D) Brass
10. The state of matter with highest kinetic energy is:
    - A) Solid
    - B) Liquid
    - C) Gas
    - D) Glass

### Question 5 [10 Marks]
- (a) Differentiate between a physical and chemical change on 3 points. [3 Marks]
- (b) Explain why burning of a candle is considered both a physical and chemical change. [3 Marks]
- (c) Define atomicity. Give one example of a monoatomic, diatomic, and triatomic gas. [4 Marks]

- (a) Explain the kinetic molecular theory of matter. State three postulates. [3 Marks]
- (b) Describe an experiment to separate a mixture of iron filings, ammonium chloride, and sand. [4 Marks]
- (c) Why does temperature remain constant during the melting of ice? [3 Marks]

  - (i) State number of protons, neutrons, and electrons. [3 Marks]
  - (ii) Write its electronic configuration and valency. [2 Marks]

  $$AgNO_3 + NaCl \rightarrow AgCl \downarrow + NaNO_3$$ [3 Marks]
