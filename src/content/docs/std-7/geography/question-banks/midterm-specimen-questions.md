## Section I (40 Marks) — Compulsory

### Question 1: Topographical Map Study [15 Marks]
Refer to a standard ICSE Topographical Map ($1:50,000$) and answer the following:
1. Give the 4-figure grid reference of a settlement located in the grid square where Easting is 23 and Northing is 45. [2 Marks]
2. What do the following conventional signs represent?
   - (a) A blue circle inside a red square. [1 Mark]
   - (b) Red broken double lines. [1 Mark]
   - (c) A small black triangle with '324' written next to it. [1 Mark]
3. If the distance between two temples on the map is $6.4\,\text{cm}$, find the ground distance in kilometers. [3 Marks]
4. What is the direction of settlement Rampur (grid 2134) from settlement Mohanpur (grid 2538)? [2 Marks]
5. State the meaning of the terms:
   - (a) Spot height [2 Marks]
   - (b) Bench mark [1 Mark]
   - (c) Form lines [2 Marks]

### Question 2: Objective & Definitions [15 Marks]
1. Name the process of peeling off of outer rock layers due to differential heating and cooling.
2. What is the term for the organic matter formed by decomposition of dead plants and animals in soil?
3. Name the mountain range separating Europe from Asia.
4. Name the longest river in Europe.
5. What type of weathering is caused by carbon dioxide dissolved in rainwater?
6. Which horizon of the soil profile is richest in humus?
7. Name the capital of Switzerland.
8. State two reasons why Switzerland is famous for precision watchmaking.
9. Name the highest peak in the Alps.
10. What is an oxbow lake?

### Question 3: Fill in the Blanks [10 Marks]
1. The $RF$ of a map where $2\,\text{cm}$ represents $1\,\text{km}$ is ______.
2. ______ weathering is dominant in hot, arid desert regions.
3. The Rhine river originates in the ______ and drains into the ______ Sea.
4. Horizon ______ in a soil profile consists of unweathered parent bedrock.
5. Switzerland is a landlocked country situated in ______ Europe.

## Section II (40 Marks) — Attempt Any 4 Questions

### Question 4 [10 Marks]
- (a) Explain mechanical weathering. Describe the process of frost action (freeze-thaw). [4 Marks]
- (b) How do burrowing animals and plant roots contribute to biological weathering? [3 Marks]
- (c) Differentiate between weathering and erosion. [3 Marks]

### Question 5 [10 Marks]
- (a) Draw a neat labeled diagram of a soil profile showing Horizons O, A, B, and C. [4 Marks]
- (b) State any three measures to prevent soil erosion on sloping hill slopes. [3 Marks]
- (c) Why is topsoil considered the most vital layer for agriculture? [3 Marks]

### Question 6 [10 Marks]
- (a) Describe the four major physical divisions of Europe. [4 Marks]
- (b) Explain why Western Europe has an oceanic climate with mild winters and cool summers. [3 Marks]
- (c) What is the North Atlantic Drift and how does it influence European ports? [3 Marks]

### Question 7 [10 Marks]
- (a) Discuss the economic importance of dairy farming and tourism in Switzerland. [4 Marks]
- (b) Why is Switzerland known as the 'Playground of Europe'? [3 Marks]
- (c) Name two major lakes in Switzerland. [3 Marks]
