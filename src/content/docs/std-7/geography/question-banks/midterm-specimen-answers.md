## Section II (40 Marks)

### Question 4 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Frost Action**: Water enters rock joints and fissures during the day. At night, temperature falls below freezing, water freezes into ice and expands by ~9% in volume. This exerts immense pressure ($>2000\,\text{kg/cm}^2$) on crack walls, shattering the rock into angular scree/talus fragments. [4 Marks]
- (b) **Biological Weathering**: Tree roots penetrate rock crevices; as roots thicken, they exert wedging pressure that widens cracks and splits rocks. Burrowing animals (rodents, earthworms, insects) loosen soil and expose sub-surface rocks to atmospheric air and water. [3 Marks]
- (c) **Weathering vs Erosion**: Weathering is in-situ disintegration and decomposition of rocks without transport; Erosion is the detachment and active transport of weathered debris by agents like wind, water, and glaciers. [3 Marks]

### Question 5 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Soil Profile Diagram**:
  - Horizon O: Organic litter. [1 Mark]
  - Horizon A: Dark topsoil rich in humus and soil organisms. [1 Mark]
  - Horizon B: Subsoil with clay and accumulated minerals. [1 Mark]
  - Horizon C: Weathered parent material above Bedrock (R). [1 Mark]
- (b) **Soil Conservation**: Terrace farming, contour bunding, planting shelter belts / afforestation. [3 Marks]
- (c) **Topsoil Importance**: Contains essential plant nutrients, moisture, humus, and microbial life required for seed germination and crop root development. [3 Marks]

### Question 6 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **European Physical Divisions**:
  - 1. Northwestern Highlands (ancient weathered mountains: Scandinavian range, Scottish Highlands). [1 Mark]
  - 2. North European Plains (vast fertile agricultural lowland from France to Urals). [1 Mark]
  - 3. Central Uplands (plateaus: Massif Central, Black Forest). [1 Mark]
  - 4. Alpine Mountain System (young fold mountains: Alps, Pyrenees, Apennines, Carpathians). [1 Mark]
- (b) **Oceanic Climate**: Warm Westerly winds blowing across the Atlantic Ocean bring abundant moisture and moderate temperatures, preventing severe winter freezing. [3 Marks]
- (c) **North Atlantic Drift**: A warm ocean current branching from the Gulf Stream. It keeps Western European coastal ports (e.g. Bergen, Murmansk) ice-free throughout the winter. [3 Marks]

### Question 7 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Switzerland Economy**:
  - Dairy farming: Alpine pastures (Alps) during summer support high-yield Swiss dairy cattle producing world-famous cheeses (Emmental, Gruyère) and milk chocolates. [2 Marks]
  - Tourism: Scenic Alpine peaks (Matterhorn, Jungfrau), glaciers, lakes, and winter ski resorts provide major foreign exchange and employment. [2 Marks]
- (b) **Playground of Europe**: Coined because of Switzerland's world-class facilities for mountaineering, skiing, ice skating, hiking, and health sanatoriums amidst pristine Alpine scenery. [3 Marks]
- (c) **Lakes**: Lake Geneva, Lake Lucerne, Lake Zurich. [3 Marks]
