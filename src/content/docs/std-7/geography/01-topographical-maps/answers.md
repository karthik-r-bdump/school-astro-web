## Section A: Multiple Choice Solutions (1 Mark Each)
1. **Question**: Yellow color represents:
   - **Answer**: **B) Cultivable agricultural land** [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: First three digits indicate:
   - **Answer**: **B) The Easting and its tenths division** [1 Mark]
3. **Question**: Contours very close together represent:
   - **Answer**: **B) A steep slope** [1 Mark]

---

## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Eastings and Northings:
   - **Answer**: Eastings are vertical grid lines numbered from West to East. Northings are horizontal grid lines numbered from South to North. [2 Marks]
2. **Question**: 4-figure vs 6-figure:
   - **Answer**: A 4-figure grid reference locates a full $1\,\text{km}^2$ grid square; a 6-figure grid reference pinpoints an exact location to within $100\,\text{m}$ by dividing the square into tenths. [2 Marks]
3. **Question**: Colors:
   - (a) Green: Forest / Vegetation [0.5 Mark]
   - (b) Blue: Perennial water bodies [0.5 Mark]
   - (c) Red: Settlements / Roads [0.5 Mark]
   - (d) Brown: Contours / Elevation [0.5 Mark]
4. **Question**: Contour Interval:
   - **Answer**: The constant vertical elevation difference between two consecutive contour lines on a topographical map. [2 Marks]
5. **Question**: Representative Fraction:
   - **Answer**: A fraction expressing the ratio of map distance to actual ground distance in the same units (e.g., $1:50,000$ means $1\,\text{cm}$ on map represents $50,000\,\text{cm}$ or $500\,\text{m}$ on ground). [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Scale calculation:
   - (a) $RF = 1:50,000 \Rightarrow 1\,\text{cm} = 50,000\,\text{cm} = 500\,\text{m} = 0.5\,\text{km}$. [1.5 Marks]
     - For $4\,\text{cm}$: $4 \times 0.5 = 2.0\,\text{km}$ on ground. [1.5 Marks]
   - (b) A verbal scale expresses scale in words (e.g. $1\,\text{cm} = 1\,\text{km}$); a linear scale represents distance graphically using a calibrated divided line that remains accurate if the map is resized. [2 Marks]
2. **Question**: Contour patterns:
   - (a) Conical hill: Concentric, roughly circular contour rings with values increasing towards the center. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Steep slope: Contours closely spaced; Gentle slope: Contours widely spaced. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Plateau: Outer contours close together showing steep sides, with a wide flat top containing few or widely spaced contours. [1 Mark]
