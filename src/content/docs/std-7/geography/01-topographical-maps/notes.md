# Chapter 1: Topographical Maps

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Definition of topographical maps: Large-scale maps depicting both natural and man-made features in detail.
- Grid Reference: Eastings (vertical lines numbered from west to east) and Northings (horizontal lines numbered from south to north).
- 4-figure and 6-figure grid references.
- Conventional signs and symbols (Colors: Blue for water, Green for vegetation, Yellow for cultivable land, Red for settlements/roads, Brown for contours/sand).
- Scale: Verbal scale, Linear scale, Representative Fraction ($RF$).
- Contour lines: Characteristics, spacing, and landform representations (Steep slope, Gentle slope, Hill, Plateau, Ridge, Valley).

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Grid References (Eastings & Northings)
- **Eastings**: Vertical lines running North-South whose numerical values increase towards the **East** (read first).
- **Northings**: Horizontal lines running East-West whose numerical values increase towards the **North** (read second).
- **4-Figure Grid Reference**: Identifies a $1\,\text{km} \times 1\,\text{km}$ grid square.
  - Rule: Read the Easting of the southwest corner of the square, then the Northing of the southwest corner.
- **6-Figure Grid Reference**: Identifies the exact location of a feature within a grid square with $100\,\text{m}$ accuracy.
  - Rule: Divide the square into tenths along Easting and Northing. Format: $EEeNNn$.

### 2. Conventional Colors on Toposheets
- **Blue**: Perennial water bodies, rivers, wells, tanks.
- **Green**: Forested areas, orchards, jungle, grassland.
- **Yellow**: Cultivable agricultural land.
- **Red**: Settlements, huts, pucca/kuccha roads, cart tracks.
- **Brown**: Contour lines, spot heights, sand dunes.
- **Black**: Railway lines, telephone lines, boundary markers, dry streams.

### 3. Contour Lines & Slopes
- **Contour Line**: An imaginary line connecting points of equal elevation above mean sea level.
- **Contour Interval ($CI$)**: The constant vertical distance between two successive contour lines (usually $20\,\text{m}$ on $1:50,000$ toposheets).
- **Slope Types**:
  - **Steep Slope**: Contours drawn very close together.
  - **Gentle Slope**: Contours spaced widely apart.
  - **Uniform Slope**: Contours spaced evenly.
  - **Conical Hill**: Concentric, roughly circular contours with values increasing inward.
