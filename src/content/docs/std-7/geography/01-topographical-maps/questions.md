## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. On a topographical sheet, yellow color represents:
   - A) Forest cover
   - B) Cultivable agricultural land
   - C) Water body
   - D) Waste land

2. In a 6-figure grid reference, the first three digits indicate:
   - A) The Northing
   - B) The Easting and its tenths division
   - C) The elevation
   - D) The contour interval

3. When contour lines are placed very close to each other, they represent:
   - A) A gentle slope
   - B) A steep slope
   - C) A flat plain
   - D) A plateau

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. What are Eastings and Northings?
2. Explain the difference between a 4-figure and a 6-figure grid reference.
3. State what the following colors indicate on an ICSE toposheet:
   - (a) Green
   - (b) Blue
   - (c) Red
   - (d) Brown
4. Define 'Contour Interval'.
5. What is a Representative Fraction ($RF$)? Give an example.

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. With reference to scale and map measurements:
   - (a) If the $RF$ of a map is $1:50,000$, calculate the actual ground distance corresponding to $4\,\text{cm}$ on the map. [3 Marks]
   - (b) Differentiate between a verbal scale and a linear scale. [2 Marks]

2. Draw and describe contour patterns representing:
   - (a) A conical hill. [2 Marks]
   - (b) A steep slope vs a gentle slope. [2 Marks]
   - (c) A plateau. [1 Mark]
