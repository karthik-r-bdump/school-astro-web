## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)

   - A) Carbon dioxide
   - B) Nitrogen
   - C) Oxygen
   - D) Hydrogen

2. Why is a leaf boiled in methylated spirit (alcohol) over a water bath during the starch test?
   - A) To kill the protoplasm
   - B) To soften the leaf
   - C) To dissolve chlorophyll safely without catching fire
   - D) To digest starch into glucose

3. The light-trapping pigment chlorophyll is located in the:
   - A) Stroma
   - B) Thylakoid membrane
   - C) Outer chloroplast membrane

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. An experiment was set up to prove that light is necessary for photosynthesis:
   - (a) Describe the procedure of covering a portion of a destarched leaf with black paper. [2 Marks]
   - (b) What will be the observation when this leaf is tested with iodine solution after 6 hours of sun exposure? [2 Marks]
   - (c) Give the scientific reason for this observation. [1 Mark]

2. Explain how photosynthesis helps maintain ecological balance on Earth:
   - (a) Role as the universal food source. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Role in atmospheric gas regulation. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Mention one anthropogenic factor disrupting this balance. [1 Mark]
