# Chapter 2: Photosynthesis

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Definition of photosynthesis: Biochemical process by which green plants synthesize glucose from carbon dioxide and water using sunlight trapped by chlorophyll.
- Overall balanced chemical equation: $6CO_2 + 12H_2O \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6H_2O + 6O_2 \uparrow$.
- Essential factors: Sunlight, Chlorophyll, Carbon Dioxide, and Water.
- Significance: Primary source of food for all heterotrophs; maintains oxygen-carbon dioxide balance in the atmosphere.
- Experiments: Testing a leaf for starch (destarching, boiling in water, boiling in alcohol over water bath, iodine test).

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Site of Photosynthesis: Chloroplast
- Located primarily in the **mesophyll cells** (palisade and spongy parenchyma) of green leaves.
- Chloroplast contains disc-like structures called **thylakoids** stacked into **grana**, suspended in the fluid matrix called **stroma**.
- **Chlorophyll** is the green pigment located on the thylakoid membranes that absorbs blue and red wavelengths of light.

### 2. Steps in the Starch Test on a Leaf
1. **Destarching**: Keep the potted plant in complete darkness for 24–48 hours to empty leaves of previously formed starch (transported as sucrose to storage organs).
2. **Boiling in Water**: Kills the leaf cells, breaks cell membranes, and makes the leaf permeable to iodine solution.
3. **Boiling in Ethanol (Water Bath)**:
   - Ethanol extracts the green pigment chlorophyll (leaf turns pale white/cream).
   - *Safety Note*: Ethanol is highly inflammable, so it MUST be heated in a water bath, never directly over a flame!
4. **Rinsing in Warm Water**: Softens the brittle leaf.
5. **Adding Iodine Solution**:
   - Starch present $\rightarrow$ Turns **blue-black**.
   - Starch absent $\rightarrow$ Turns **brownish-yellow**.

### 3. Factors Affecting Photosynthesis
- **Light Intensity**: Rate increases up to a saturation point.
- **Carbon Dioxide Concentration**: Rate increases up to approximately 0.05% $CO_2$.
- **Temperature**: Optimum is between $25^\circ\text{C}$ and $35^\circ\text{C}$. Enzymes denature above $40^\circ\text{C}$.
