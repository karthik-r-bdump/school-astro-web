## Section A: Multiple Choice Solutions (1 Mark Each)
1. **Question**: The gas liberated during photosynthesis is:
   - **Answer**: **C) Oxygen**
   - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct option.
   - *Explanation*: Photolysis of water in the presence of light releases $O_2$ into the atmosphere.

2. **Question**: Why is a leaf boiled in methylated spirit over a water bath?
   - **Answer**: **C) To dissolve chlorophyll safely without catching fire**
   - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct option.
   - *Explanation*: Ethanol is inflammable; a water bath ensures safe heating while dissolving the green pigment so color changes can be observed.

3. **Question**: The light-trapping pigment chlorophyll is located in the:
   - **Answer**: **B) Thylakoid membrane**
   - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct option.

## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Write the balanced chemical equation representing photosynthesis.
   - **Answer**:
     $$6CO_2 + 12H_2O \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6H_2O + 6O_2 \uparrow$$
     - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct reactants and products; 1 mark for correct coefficients and reaction conditions (Sunlight & Chlorophyll).

2. **Question**: Why is it necessary to destarch a plant before performing an experiment on photosynthesis?
   - **Answer**:
     - To ensure that any starch detected at the end of the experiment was synthesized *during* the experiment, not beforehand. [2 Marks]

3. **Question**: State two adaptations of a leaf that make it well-suited for photosynthesis.
   - **Answer**:
     - Large surface area to absorb maximum sunlight. [1 Mark]
     - Numerous stomata on the lower surface for rapid gas exchange ($CO_2$ in, $O_2$ out). [1 Mark]

4. **Question**: What happens when iodine solution is added to a boiled leaf containing starch versus a destarched leaf?
   - **Answer**:
     - Leaf with starch turns **blue-black**. [1 Mark]
     - Destarched leaf turns **brown/yellowish-brown**. [1 Mark]

5. **Question**: State the role of stomata in photosynthesis.
   - **Answer**:
     - Stomata are microscopic pores regulated by guard cells that allow diffusion of carbon dioxide into the leaf and release of oxygen and water vapor. [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Experiment to prove light is necessary for photosynthesis:
   - (a) Procedure: Destarch a potted plant. Attach a strip of black paper across both surfaces of a leaf using paper clips. Place the plant in sunlight for 4–6 hours. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Observation: The uncovered part turns blue-black with iodine; the covered part remains brown. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Reason: The covered part did not receive light, preventing photosynthesis and starch synthesis. [1 Mark]

2. **Question**: Ecological balance:
   - (a) Universal food source: Plants are primary producers; solar energy converted into chemical energy supports all trophic levels (herbivores, carnivores). [2 Marks]
   - (b) Gas regulation: Consumes $CO_2$ (mitigating greenhouse effect) and replenishes $O_2$ required for aerobic respiration of living organisms. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Disruption: Deforestation or excessive burning of fossil fuels. [1 Mark]
