# Chapter 4: Human Excretory System

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Definition of excretion: Removal of toxic metabolic nitrogenous waste products from the body.
- Primary excretory organs: Kidneys, Ureters, Urinary Bladder, Urethra.
- Structure of kidney: Cortex, Medulla, Renal pelvis.
- Structure and function of a Nephron (Renal tubule, Bowman's capsule, Glomerulus).
- Formation of urine: Ultrafiltration, Selective reabsorption, Tubular secretion.

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Human Excretory System Components
- **Kidneys**: Pair of bean-shaped, reddish-brown organs on either side of the vertebral column.
- **Ureters**: Muscular tubes carrying urine from renal pelvis to bladder by peristalsis.
- **Urinary Bladder**: Distensible muscular sac storing urine temporarily.
- **Urethra**: Canal carrying urine from bladder to exterior.

### 2. The Nephron: Structural & Functional Unit
- **Malpighian Body**:
  - **Glomerulus**: Knot of capillaries receiving blood from afferent arteriole and draining into narrower efferent arteriole.
  - **Bowman's Capsule**: Double-walled cup surrounding glomerulus.
- **Renal Tubule**:
  - Proximal Convoluted Tubule (PCT)
  - Loop of Henle (descending and ascending limbs)
  - Distal Convoluted Tubule (DCT)
  - Collecting Duct

### 3. Urine Formation
1. **Ultrafiltration**: High hydrostatic pressure in glomerulus forces water, glucose, salts, and urea into Bowman's capsule as glomerular filtrate. Blood cells and large proteins remain in blood.
2. **Selective Reabsorption**: In PCT and Loop of Henle, essential substances (all glucose, amino acids, necessary water, and ions) are reabsorbed into blood capillaries.
3. **Tubular Secretion**: Extra wastes (potassium ions, hydrogen ions, creatinine) are secreted from capillaries into DCT.
