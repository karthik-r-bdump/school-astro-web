## Section A: Multiple Choice Solutions (1 Mark Each)
1. **Question**: The site of aerobic cellular respiration is:
   - **Answer**: **B) Mitochondria**
   - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct option.

2. **Question**: The end product of anaerobic respiration in human muscle is:
   - **Answer**: **C) Lactic acid**
   - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct option.

3. **Question**: The energy currency of the cell is:
   - **Answer**: **B) ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate)**
   - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct option.

## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Write the balanced equation for aerobic respiration.
   - **Answer**:
     $$C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + 38\,\text{ATP}$$
     - *Marking*: 1 mark for correct equation; 1 mark for stating energy/ATP release.

2. **Question**: Aerobic vs Anaerobic respiration:
   - (a) **Oxygen requirement**: Aerobic requires oxygen; Anaerobic occurs in absence of oxygen. [1 Mark]
   - (b) **ATP yield**: Aerobic yields 38 ATP per glucose; Anaerobic yields only 2 ATP. [1 Mark]

3. **Question**: Respiratory organs:
   - (a) Earthworm: Moist skin (cutaneous) [0.5 Mark]
   - (b) Cockroach: Spiracles and tracheae [0.5 Mark]
   - (c) Fish: Gills [0.5 Mark]
   - (d) Frog on land: Lungs and moist buccal cavity [0.5 Mark]

4. **Question**: Why do muscle cramps occur after strenuous exercise?
   - **Answer**: Due to insufficient oxygen supply, muscles respire anaerobically, producing **lactic acid** which accumulates and causes muscle fatigue and cramps. [2 Marks]

5. **Question**: Respiration vs Combustion:
   - Respiration is a slow, multi-step enzymatic process at body temperature releasing energy as ATP. [1 Mark]
   - Combustion is a rapid, non-enzymatic chemical reaction occurring at high temperature releasing energy as heat and light. [1 Mark]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Experiment demonstrating heat release by germinating seeds:
   - (a) Setup: Place moist germinating seeds in Thermos Flask A. Insert a thermometer. Seal with cotton wool. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Control: Place boiled seeds soaked in disinfectant (carbolic acid) in Thermos Flask B. Boiled seeds are dead and prevent bacterial decomposition. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Observation: Thermometer in Flask A shows a marked rise in temperature, proving respiration releases heat. [1 Mark]

2. **Question**: Mechanism of breathing:
   - (a) Inhalation: Diaphragm contracts and flattens; external intercostal muscles contract moving ribs upward and outward. Thoracic volume increases, pressure drops, air rushes in. [2 Marks]
   - (b) Exhalation: Diaphragm relaxes and arches upward (dome-shaped); ribs move down and in. Thoracic volume decreases, pressure rises, air is expelled. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Alveoli: Thin-walled, moist, surrounded by capillaries, providing vast surface area for diffusion of $O_2$ and $CO_2$. [1 Mark]
