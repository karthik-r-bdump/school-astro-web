# Chapter 3: Respiration in Plants and Animals

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Definition of respiration: Catabolic process of cellular oxidation of glucose releasing energy in the form of ATP.
- Types: Aerobic respiration (with oxygen) and Anaerobic respiration (without oxygen / fermentation).
- Chemical equations for both types.
- Comparison: Respiration vs Photosynthesis, Respiration vs Combustion.
- Respiratory organs in animals (Skin, Tracheae, Gills, Lungs).

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Cellular Respiration
- **Aerobic Respiration**:
  $$C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + 38\,\text{ATP}$$
  - Takes place in mitochondria.
  - Complete oxidation of glucose producing maximum energy.
- **Anaerobic Respiration (Fermentation)**:
  - In Yeast (Alcoholic fermentation):
    $$C_6H_{12}O_6 \rightarrow 2C_2H_5OH + 2CO_2 + 2\,\text{ATP}$$
  - In human skeletal muscles during vigorous exercise (Lactic acid fermentation):
    $$C_6H_{12}O_6 \rightarrow 2\,\text{Lactic Acid} + 2\,\text{ATP}$$

### 2. Comparison Table: Respiration vs Photosynthesis
| Feature | Respiration | Photosynthesis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature** | Catabolic (destructive/breakdown) | Anabolic (constructive/building up) |
| **Energy** | Releases energy (ATP) | Absorbs solar energy |
| **Raw Materials** | Glucose and Oxygen | Carbon dioxide and Water |
| **By-products** | $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ | $O_2$ and $H_2O$ |
| **Occurrence** | All living cells, day and night | Only chlorophyllous cells, in light |
