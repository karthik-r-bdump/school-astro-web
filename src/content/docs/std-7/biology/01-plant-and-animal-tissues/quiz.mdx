<Quiz title="Types of Plant Tissues" autoNumber>
Which type of plant tissue consists of actively dividing cells with dense cytoplasm and large nuclei?

- [x] Meristematic tissue
- [ ] Permanent tissue
- [ ] Sclerenchyma tissue
- [ ] Parenchyma tissue

**Explanation:** Meristematic tissue consists of young, actively dividing cells located at growing tips (apical), nodes (intercalary), and girth (lateral). They possess thin cellulose walls, dense cytoplasm, and prominent nuclei with little or no vacuolation.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Vascular Tissues in Plants" autoNumber>
Which of the following tissues is responsible for the conduction of water and dissolved minerals from roots to leaves?

- [ ] Phloem
- [x] Xylem
- [ ] Collenchyma
- [ ] Cambium

**Explanation:** Xylem is the complex permanent vascular tissue responsible for unidirectional upward transport of water and mineral salts from the soil to the leaves. Phloem transports synthesized food bidirectionally.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Components of Xylem" autoNumber>
Which of the following elements are present in xylem tissue? (Select all that apply)

- [x] Vessels (Tracheae)
- [x] Tracheids
- [ ] Sieve tubes
- [x] Xylem parenchyma
- [ ] Companion cells

**Explanation:** Xylem consists of four elements: Tracheids, Vessels, Xylem parenchyma (only living component), and Xylem fibres. Sieve tubes and companion cells are components of phloem tissue.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Nervous System Unit" autoNumber>
The fundamental structural and functional unit of the nervous system is the [[neuron]].

**Explanation:** Neurons (nerve cells) consist of a cell body (cyton), dendrites that receive impulses, and a long axon that transmits electrical impulses to other neurons or effector organs.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Connective Tissues" autoNumber>
Which connective tissue connects a muscle to a bone?

- [ ] Ligament
  > Ligaments connect bone to bone.
- [x] Tendon
- [ ] Cartilage
  > Cartilage cushions joints and reduces friction.
- [ ] Areolar tissue

**Explanation:** Tendons are tough, inelastic fibrous connective tissues that join skeletal muscles to bones. Ligaments are elastic and join bone to bone at joints.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Fluid Connective Tissue" autoNumber>
Which of the following is a fluid connective tissue that lacks fibres and contains cells suspended in plasma?

- [x] Blood
- [ ] Adipose tissue
- [ ] Areolar tissue
- [ ] Bone

**Explanation:** Blood is a fluid connective tissue composed of liquid matrix (plasma) and formed elements (RBCs, WBCs, and platelets). It circulates oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and metabolic wastes.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Muscular Tissue Characteristics" autoNumber>
Which type of muscle tissue is involuntary, branched, and possesses intercalated discs?

- [ ] Striated skeletal muscle
- [ ] Smooth visceral muscle
- [x] Cardiac muscle
- [ ] Voluntary muscle

**Explanation:** Cardiac muscles are found exclusively in the heart wall. They are involuntary, cylindrical, branched, and contract rhythmically without fatigue throughout life.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Sclerenchyma Cell Walls" autoNumber>
Dead plant cells with walls heavily thickened by [[lignin]] provide mechanical strength to plant parts.

**Explanation:** Sclerenchyma cells are dead at maturity and possess uniform, heavily lignified secondary cell walls providing rigidity, mechanical support, and tensile strength (e.g., coconut husk, walnut shells).
</Quiz>
