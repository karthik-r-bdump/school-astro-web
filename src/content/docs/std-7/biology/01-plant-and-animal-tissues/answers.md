## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Meristematic vs Permanent:
   - Meristematic consists of actively dividing undifferentiated cells with thin walls; Permanent consists of specialized non-dividing mature cells. [2 Marks]
2. **Question**: Adipose tissue functions:
   - Stores fat globules; provides thermal insulation against heat loss and cushions vital organs. [2 Marks]
3. **Question**: Absence of vacuoles:
   - Cells are actively dividing and do not need to store food or wastes. [2 Marks]
4. **Question**: Tendon vs Ligament:
   - Tendons connect muscle to bone; Ligaments connect bone to bone at joints. [2 Marks]
5. **Question**: Four phloem components:
   - Sieve tubes, Companion cells, Phloem parenchyma, Phloem fibres. [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Meristem types:
   - (a) Apical: Growth in length at root/shoot tips; Lateral (cambium): Growth in thickness/girth; Intercalary: Regrowth at nodes/internodes. [3 Marks]
   - (b) Sclerenchyma cell walls are heavily deposited with lignin, a rigid cementing chemical that resists bending and compression. [2 Marks]
2. **Question**: Muscle & Nerve:
   - (a) Striated is cylindrical, unbranched, voluntary; Cardiac is cylindrical, branched, involuntary with rhythmic contraction. [3 Marks]
   - (b) Nervous tissue receives stimuli and transmits electrical nerve impulses. Neuron consists of cyton, dendrites, and axon. [2 Marks]
