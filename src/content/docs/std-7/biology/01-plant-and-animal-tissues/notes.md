# Chapter 1: Plant and Animal Tissues

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Definition of tissue: A group of similar cells performing a specific function.
- Plant Tissues: Meristematic (apical, lateral, intercalary) and Permanent (simple: parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma; complex: xylem, phloem).
- Animal Tissues: Epithelial, Connective, Muscular, Nervous.

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Plant Tissues
- **Meristematic**: Cells are small, thin-walled, actively dividing, with dense cytoplasm and no vacuoles. Located at root/shoot tips (apical), cambium (lateral), and nodes (intercalary).
- **Permanent**:
  - *Parenchyma*: Living, thin-walled; storage and photosynthesis (chlorenchyma).
  - *Collenchyma*: Living, corners thickened with pectin; mechanical flexibility.
  - *Sclerenchyma*: Dead, heavily lignified walls, narrow lumen; rigidity and strength.
  - *Xylem*: Tracheids, vessels, xylem fibres (dead), xylem parenchyma (living). Transports water and minerals upward.
  - *Phloem*: Sieve tubes, companion cells, phloem parenchyma (living), phloem fibres (dead). Translocates food bidirectionally.

### 2. Animal Tissues
- **Epithelial**: Squamous (alveoli, mouth), Cuboidal (kidney tubules), Columnar (intestine), Ciliated (trachea).
- **Connective**: Areolar, Adipose (stores fat), Bone (osteocytes), Cartilage (chondrocytes), Blood (plasma, RBC, WBC, platelets).
- **Muscular**: Striated (voluntary), Unstriated (involuntary), Cardiac (involuntary, branched, never fatigues).
- **Nervous**: Neuron (cyton, dendrites, axon with myelin sheath).
