## Section II (40 Marks)

### Question 5 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Parenchyma vs Sclerenchyma** [3 Marks]:
  - Parenchyma is living; Sclerenchyma is dead at maturity. [1 Mark]
  - Parenchyma cell wall is thin (cellulose); Sclerenchyma cell wall is heavily lignified. [1 Mark]
  - Parenchyma has large central vacuole and intercellular spaces; Sclerenchyma has narrow lumen and no intercellular spaces. [1 Mark]
- (b) **Photosynthesis Equation** [2 Marks]:
  $$6CO_2 + 12H_2O \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6H_2O + 6O_2 \uparrow$$
  [1 Mark for reactants/products, 1 Mark for conditions & balancing].
- (c) **Boiling leaf in alcohol** [3 Marks]:
  - Leaf is placed in a test tube containing ethanol, which is placed inside a beaker of boiling water (water bath). [2 Marks]
  - *Precaution*: Alcohol is highly flammable; never heat directly over open flame. [1 Mark]
- (d) **Functions of plasma** [2 Marks]:
  - Transports nutrients (glucose, amino acids) and hormones. [1 Mark]
  - Carries metabolic wastes (urea, $CO_2$) to excretory organs. [1 Mark]

### Question 6 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Neuron Structure** [4 Marks]:
  - Cyton (cell body) with nucleus and granular cytoplasm. [1 Mark]
  - Dendrites: branched projections carrying impulses toward cyton. [1 Mark]
  - Axon: long nerve fibre with myelin sheath carrying impulses away. [1 Mark]
  - Diagram / terminal arborization with synaptic knob. [1 Mark]
- (b) **Stomata** [3 Marks]:
  - Microscopic apertures in the leaf epidermis flanked by two guard cells. [1 Mark]
  - Function 1: Exchange of respiratory and photosynthetic gases ($CO_2, O_2$). [1 Mark]
  - Function 2: Transpiration (loss of water vapor producing cooling effect). [1 Mark]
- (c) **Muscle Cramps** [3 Marks]:
  - During intense exercise, oxygen demand exceeds supply. [1 Mark]
  - Muscles undergo anaerobic respiration forming lactic acid. [1 Mark]
  - Accumulation of lactic acid inhibits muscle contraction, causing painful cramps. [1 Mark]

### Question 7 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Urine Formation Steps** [5 Marks]:
  - 1. Ultrafiltration: High pressure in glomerulus filters water and solutes into Bowman's capsule as glomerular filtrate. [2 Marks]
  - 2. Selective Reabsorption: PCT and Loop of Henle reabsorb all glucose, amino acids, essential ions, and ~80% water into peritubular capillaries. [2 Marks]
  - 3. Tubular Secretion: Capillaries actively secrete $H^+, K^+$ and creatinine into distal tubule. [1 Mark]
- (b) **Aerobic vs Anaerobic** [3 Marks]:
  - Oxygen: Required in aerobic; absent in anaerobic. [1 Mark]
  - End products: $CO_2 + H_2O$ vs Ethyl alcohol + $CO_2$ (or Lactic acid). [1 Mark]
  - Energy: 38 ATP per glucose vs 2 ATP per glucose. [1 Mark]
- (c) **Other excretory organs** [2 Marks]:
  - Lungs (excrete $CO_2$ and water vapor). [1 Mark]
  - Skin / sweat glands (excrete sweat containing salts and traces of urea). [1 Mark]

### Question 8 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Breathing Mechanism** [4 Marks]:
  - Inhalation: Diaphragm contracts and flattens; external intercostals pull ribs upward/outward; thoracic cavity expands, air enters. [2 Marks]
  - Exhalation: Diaphragm relaxes into dome shape; ribs move down/inward; thoracic volume decreases, air pushed out. [2 Marks]
- (b) **Destarching Reason** [3 Marks]:
  - Plants store previously synthesized food as starch. If not removed by placing the plant in darkness for 24–48 hours, residual starch will give a false positive test with iodine, obscuring whether light was strictly needed. [3 Marks]
- (c) **Tissue Definition & 4 Animal Types** [3 Marks]:
  - Definition: Group of similar cells specialized to perform a common function. [1 Mark]
  - 4 Types: Epithelial, Connective, Muscular, Nervous tissue. [2 Marks]
