## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. Who among the following rulers introduced the court customs of Sijdah and Paibos?
   - A) Iltutmish
   - B) Alauddin Khalji
   - C) Ghiyas-ud-din Balban
   - D) Babur

2. Which Sultan was known as 'Lakhbaksh' for his immense generosity?
   - A) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
   - B) Razia Sultan
   - C) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
   - D) Firoz Shah Tughlaq

3. Malik Kafur was the trusted general of:
   - A) Balban
   - B) Alauddin Khalji
   - C) Iltutmish
   - D) Ibrahim Lodi

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. Why was Razia Sultan opposed by the Turkish nobles (Chahalgani)?
2. What were the practices of Sijdah and Paibos introduced by Balban?
3. State two reasons why Muhammad bin Tughlaq decided to transfer his capital from Delhi to Daulatabad.
4. Explain the terms 'Dagh' and 'Chehra' introduced by Alauddin Khalji.
