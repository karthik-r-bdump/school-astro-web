## Part I: Civics (30 Marks)

### Question 1: Short Answer Questions [10 Marks]
1. Who was the permanent President of the Constituent Assembly?
2. Mention the date on which the Constitution was adopted and the date it was enacted.
3. What is meant by Universal Adult Suffrage?
4. Name any two Fundamental Duties enshrined in the Indian Constitution.
5. Define the term 'Preamble'.

### Question 2: Structured Civics Questions [20 Marks]
- (a) Explain the significance of the following terms in the Preamble:
  - (i) Sovereign [2 Marks]
  - (ii) Socialist [2 Marks]
  - (iii) Secular [2 Marks]
- (b) With reference to Fundamental Rights:
  - (i) Explain the Right to Equality and state two provisions under it. [4 Marks]
  - (ii) Why is the Right to Constitutional Remedies considered vital? [3 Marks]
  - (iii) What is the Right against Exploitation? [3 Marks]
- (c) Differentiate between Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles of State Policy. [4 Marks]

## Part II: History (50 Marks)

### Question 3: Objective Questions [10 Marks]
1. Who founded the Slave Dynasty?
2. Which Sultan introduced the silver coin *Tanka* and copper coin *Jital*?
3. Name the first woman ruler of the Delhi Sultanate.
4. Who introduced the military practices of *Dagh* and *Chehra*?
5. Name the general who led Alauddin Khalji's southern campaigns.
6. What was the capital of the Vijayanagar Empire?
7. Name the Persian court customs introduced by Balban.
8. Why did Muhammad bin Tughlaq introduce token currency?
9. Who was the greatest ruler of the Vijayanagar Empire?
10. Name the monument built by Qutb-ud-din Aibak in Delhi.

### Question 4: Structured History Questions [40 Marks] — Attempt Any 4

#### Question 4.1: The Mamluk Dynasty [10 Marks]
- (a) Explain Balban's 'Blood and Iron' policy. [3 Marks]
- (b) What was the role of the 'Chahalgani' in the Sultanate and how did Balban destroy them? [4 Marks]
- (c) Mention two achievements of Iltutmish. [3 Marks]

#### Question 4.2: Alauddin Khalji's Administration [10 Marks]
- (a) Describe Alauddin Khalji's market control and price fixation system. [4 Marks]
- (b) Explain his military reforms and the purpose of paying soldiers in cash. [3 Marks]
- (c) How did he deal with the threat of Mongol invasions? [3 Marks]

#### Question 4.3: Muhammad bin Tughlaq's Experiments [10 Marks]
- (a) Explain why the transfer of capital from Delhi to Daulatabad failed. [4 Marks]
- (b) What was the token currency experiment and why did it collapse? [4 Marks]
- (c) What were the consequences of his taxation policy in the Doab? [2 Marks]

#### Question 4.4: The Vijayanagar and Bahmani Kingdoms [10 Marks]
- (a) Who founded the Vijayanagar Empire and when? [2 Marks]
- (b) Describe the cultural and literary contributions of Krishnadeva Raya. [4 Marks]
- (c) Name the battle in 1565 that led to the decline of the Vijayanagar Empire. Describe its outcome. [4 Marks]
