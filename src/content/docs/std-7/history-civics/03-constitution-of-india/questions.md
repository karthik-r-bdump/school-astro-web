## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. The Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constitution was:
   - A) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
   - B) Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru
   - C) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
   - D) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

2. The Indian Constitution came into effect on:
   - A) 15 August 1947
   - B) 26 November 1949
   - C) 26 January 1950
   - D) 2 October 1950

3. Which term in the Preamble implies that the Head of the State is elected and not hereditary?
   - A) Sovereign
   - B) Socialist
   - C) Republic
   - D) Secular

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. Define the term 'Constitution'.
2. What is the historical significance of choosing 26th January as the Republic Day of India?
3. Differentiate between a 'Democratic' state and a 'Republic'.
4. Why did Dr. B.R. Ambedkar describe the Right to Constitutional Remedies as the 'Heart and Soul' of the Constitution?
5. State any two Fundamental Duties of an Indian citizen.

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. With reference to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution:
   - (a) Explain the meaning of the terms 'Sovereign' and 'Secular'. [3 Marks]
   - (b) What are the four core ideals promised to all citizens in the Preamble? [2 Marks]

2. With reference to Fundamental Rights:
   - (a) Explain the Right against Exploitation. [2 Marks]
   - (b) What is meant by Right to Equality? Mention two provisions under it. [2 Marks]
   - (c) Can Fundamental Rights be suspended? If so, when? [1 Mark]
