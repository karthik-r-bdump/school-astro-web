# Class 7 History & Civics

Class 7 History covers the medieval period in India, focusing on the Turkish invasions, the Delhi Sultanate, regional Deccan kingdoms, and the architectural and cultural synthesis of medieval society. Civics explores the democratic foundations of the Constitution of India and Fundamental Rights.
