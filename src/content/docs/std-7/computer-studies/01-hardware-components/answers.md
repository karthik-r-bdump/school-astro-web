## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. ALU performs calculations and logic; CU coordinates operations. [2 Marks]
2. RAM is volatile and read/write; ROM is non-volatile and read-only. [2 Marks]
3. SMPS converts high AC voltage to safe low-voltage DC currents. [2 Marks]
4. Motherboard is the main circuit board connecting all internal parts. [2 Marks]
5. HDD has spinning magnetic platters; SSD has solid-state flash memory chips with much higher speed. [2 Marks]
