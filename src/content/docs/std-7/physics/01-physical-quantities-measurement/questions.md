## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. The SI unit of density is:
   - A) $g/cm^3$
   - B) $kg/m^3$
   - C) $g/m^3$
   - D) $kg/cm^3$

2. The density of water is maximum at:
   - A) $0^\circ\text{C}$
   - B) $4^\circ\text{C}$
   - C) $100^\circ\text{C}$
   - D) $-4^\circ\text{C}$

3. If the relative density of gold is 19.3, its density in SI units is:
   - A) $19.3\,\text{kg/m}^3$
   - B) $1930\,\text{kg/m}^3$
   - C) $19300\,\text{kg/m}^3$
   - D) $1.93\,\text{kg/m}^3$

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. Define density. State its CGS and SI units.
2. What is relative density? Why does it have no units?
3. Convert $2.5\,\text{g/cm}^3$ into $\text{kg/m}^3$. Show the calculation step.
4. Describe how a measuring cylinder is used to find the volume of an irregular stone.
5. An object of mass $180\,\text{g}$ has a volume of $20\,\text{cm}^3$. Calculate its density and state whether it will float or sink in water.

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. A piece of iron of mass $390\,\text{g}$ is immersed in a measuring cylinder containing $60\,\text{ml}$ of water. The water level rises to $110\,\text{ml}$:
   - (a) Calculate the volume of the iron piece. [1 Mark]
   - (b) Calculate the density of iron in CGS and SI units. [3 Marks]
   - (c) State its relative density. [1 Mark]

2. Explain the principle of flotation:
   - (a) When does a body float, sink, or remain suspended in a liquid? [3 Marks]
   - (b) Why does an iron nail sink in water while a massive iron ship floats? [2 Marks]
