## Section A: Multiple Choice Solutions (1 Mark Each)
1. **Question**: SI unit of density:
   - **Answer**: **B) $kg/m^3$** [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: Density of water is maximum at:
   - **Answer**: **B) $4^\circ\text{C}$** ($1000\,\text{kg/m}^3$) [1 Mark]
3. **Question**: Relative density of gold 19.3 in SI:
   - **Answer**: **C) $19300\,\text{kg/m}^3$** ($19.3 \times 1000$) [1 Mark]

## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Define density and units.
   - **Answer**: Density is mass per unit volume ($d = m/V$). SI unit: $kg/m^3$; CGS unit: $g/cm^3$. [2 Marks]
2. **Question**: Relative density and why no units:
   - **Answer**: Relative density is the ratio of the density of a substance to that of water at $4^\circ\text{C}$. Because it is a ratio of two similar quantities, the units cancel out. [2 Marks]
3. **Question**: Convert $2.5\,\text{g/cm}^3$:
   - **Answer**: $2.5 \times 1000 = 2500\,\text{kg/m}^3$. [2 Marks]
4. **Question**: Measuring volume:
   - **Answer**: Note initial water volume $V_1$. Lower stone until submerged, note volume $V_2$. Volume of stone $= V_2 - V_1$. [2 Marks]
5. **Question**: Mass 180g, Volume 20cm3:
   - **Answer**: $d = 180/20 = 9.0\,\text{g/cm}^3$. Since $9.0 > 1.0\,\text{g/cm}^3$ (water), the object will sink. [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Iron piece numerical:
   - (a) Volume: $110 - 60 = 50\,\text{cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
   - (b) Density CGS: $d = m/V = 390/50 = 7.8\,\text{g/cm}^3$. [1.5 Marks]
     - Density SI: $7.8 \times 1000 = 7800\,\text{kg/m}^3$. [1.5 Marks]
   - (c) Relative density: $7.8$ (no units). [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: Principle of flotation:
   - (a) If weight of body $>$ buoyant force (or density $>$ liquid), it sinks. If weight $<$ buoyant force, it floats. If equal, it floats fully submerged. [3 Marks]
   - (b) An iron nail has high average density and sinks. A ship is hollow with vast enclosed air, making its average density less than water. [2 Marks]
