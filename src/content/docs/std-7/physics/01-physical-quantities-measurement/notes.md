# Chapter 1: Physical Quantities and Measurement

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Measurement of volume of liquids and irregular solids using a measuring cylinder / eureka can.
- Density: Definition (mass per unit volume), formula $d = \frac{m}{V}$, SI unit ($kg/m^3$) and CGS unit ($g/cm^3$).
- Relation: $1\,\text{g/cm}^3 = 1000\,\text{kg/m}^3$.
- Relative Density: Definition, formula $RD = \frac{\text{Density of substance}}{\text{Density of water at } 4^\circ\text{C}}$, no units.
- Speed: Definition, formula $s = \frac{d}{t}$, units ($m/s, km/h$).

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Density and Relative Density
- **Density ($d$)**: Mass per unit volume of a substance.
  $$d = \frac{m}{V}$$
  - SI Unit: $kg/m^3$
  - CGS Unit: $g/cm^3$
  - Conversion: $1\,\text{g/cm}^3 = 1000\,\text{kg/m}^3$.
- **Relative Density ($RD$)**: Ratio of the density of a substance to the density of water at $4^\circ\text{C}$.
  $$RD = \frac{\text{Density of substance}}{\text{Density of water at } 4^\circ\text{C}} = \frac{\text{Mass of any volume of substance}}{\text{Mass of equal volume of water at } 4^\circ\text{C}}$$
  - Since it is a ratio of two identical physical quantities, **Relative Density has NO units**.
  - Density of water at $4^\circ\text{C} = 1.0\,\text{g/cm}^3 = 1000\,\text{kg/m}^3$.

### 2. Measuring Volume of Irregular Solids
1. Partially fill a graduated measuring cylinder with water to reading $V_1$.
2. Gently immerse the irregular insoluble solid tied with a fine thread until fully submerged.
3. Note the new water level $V_2$.
4. Volume of the irregular solid:
   $$V = V_2 - V_1$$
