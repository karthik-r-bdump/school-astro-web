## Section I (40 Marks) — Compulsory

### Question 1: Multiple Choice Questions [10 Marks]
1. The density of an object is $5\,\text{g/cm}^3$. Its relative density is:
   - A) 5000
   - B) 5
   - C) 0.5
   - D) 50
2. A train travels $180\,\text{km}$ in 3 hours. Its speed in $m/s$ is:
   - A) $60\,\text{m/s}$
   - B) $16.67\,\text{m/s}$
   - C) $50\,\text{m/s}$
   - D) $20\,\text{m/s}$
3. Work done is zero when the angle between force and displacement is:
   - A) $0^\circ$
   - B) $45^\circ$
   - C) $90^\circ$
   - D) $180^\circ$
4. An image formed by a plane mirror is always:
   - A) Real and inverted
   - B) Virtual and erect
   - C) Real and erect
   - D) Virtual and inverted
5. The kinetic energy of a body becomes 4 times when its speed is:
   - A) Halved
   - B) Doubled
   - C) Quadrupled
   - D) Unchanged
6. The phenomenon of lateral inversion is observed in:
   - A) Convex mirror
   - B) Concave mirror
   - C) Plane mirror
   - D) Prism
7. One metric ton is equal to:
   - A) $100\,\text{kg}$
   - B) $500\,\text{kg}$
   - C) $1000\,\text{kg}$
   - D) $10000\,\text{kg}$
8. A pendulum takes 20 seconds to complete 10 oscillations. Its time period is:
   - A) $0.5\,\text{s}$
   - B) $2.0\,\text{s}$
   - C) $200\,\text{s}$
   - D) $10\,\text{s}$
9. In a stretched bow, energy stored is:
   - A) Kinetic energy
   - B) Elastic potential energy
   - C) Thermal energy
   - D) Chemical energy
10. The speed of light in vacuum is:
    - A) $3 \times 10^8\,\text{m/s}$
    - B) $3 \times 10^5\,\text{m/s}$
    - C) $3 \times 10^8\,\text{km/s}$
    - D) $330\,\text{m/s}$

### Question 2: Fill in the Blanks & Formulas [10 Marks]
1. $1\,\text{g/cm}^3 = \text{______}\,\text{kg/m}^3$.
2. The formula for kinetic energy of a body of mass $m$ moving with velocity $v$ is ______.
3. The angle of incidence is always ______ to the angle of reflection.
4. Motion of the blades of a ceiling fan is an example of ______ motion.
5. The unit of work is ______.

### Question 3: Definitions & Units [10 Marks]
Define the following and state their SI units:
1. Density
2. Velocity
3. Work
4. Potential Energy
5. Frequency of oscillation

### Question 4: True / False [10 Marks]
State True or False; if false, correct the statement:
1. Relative density has the SI unit $kg/m^3$.
2. A plane mirror forms a real image of a real object.
3. Displacement can be zero even if distance traveled is not zero.
4. When a stone is thrown vertically upwards, its kinetic energy increases as it rises.
5. Light travels in a straight line in a homogeneous medium.

## Section II (40 Marks) — Attempt Any 4 Questions

### Question 5 [10 Marks]
- (a) A metallic block of dimensions $5\,\text{cm} \times 4\,\text{cm} \times 2\,\text{cm}$ has a mass of $316\,\text{g}$.
  - (i) Find the volume of the block. [2 Marks]
  - (ii) Calculate its density in CGS and SI units. [3 Marks]
- (b) State the two laws of reflection of light. [3 Marks]
- (c) What is meant by lateral inversion? Give an example. [2 Marks]

### Question 6 [10 Marks]
- (a) Differentiate between distance and displacement on 3 points. [3 Marks]
- (b) A car starts from rest and attains a velocity of $54\,\text{km/h}$ in $10\,\text{s}$. Calculate its acceleration. [4 Marks]
- (c) Name the three types of motion exhibited by a rolling bowling ball. [3 Marks]

### Question 7 [10 Marks]
- (a) State the Law of Conservation of Energy. Give two examples of energy transformations. [4 Marks]
- (b) Calculate the work done in lifting a mass of $50\,\text{kg}$ to a vertical height of $4\,\text{m}$ ($g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$). [3 Marks]
- (c) Differentiate between renewable and non-renewable energy sources. [3 Marks]
