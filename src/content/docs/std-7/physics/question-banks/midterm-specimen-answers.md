### Question 3 Solutions [10 Marks]
1. **Density**: Mass per unit volume ($d = m/V$). SI unit: $kg/m^3$. [2 Marks]
2. **Velocity**: Rate of change of displacement with time in a specific direction. SI unit: $m/s$. [2 Marks]
3. **Work**: Product of force and displacement in the direction of force ($W = F \times s$). SI unit: Joule ($J$). [2 Marks]
4. **Potential Energy**: Energy possessed by a body due to its position, state of strain, or configuration ($PE = mgh$). SI unit: Joule ($J$). [2 Marks]
5. **Frequency**: Number of complete oscillations made by a pendulum per second ($f = 1/T$). SI unit: Hertz ($Hz$). [2 Marks]

## Section II (40 Marks)

### Question 5 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Metallic Block Numerical** [5 Marks]:
  - (i) Volume $= l \times b \times h = 5 \times 4 \times 2 = 40\,\text{cm}^3$. [2 Marks]
  - (ii) Density in CGS: $d = m/V = 316/40 = 7.9\,\text{g/cm}^3$. [1.5 Marks]
    - Density in SI: $7.9 \times 1000 = 7900\,\text{kg/m}^3$. [1.5 Marks]
- (b) **Laws of Reflection** [3 Marks]:
  - 1. The incident ray, the reflected ray, and the normal at the point of incidence all lie in the same plane. [1.5 Marks]
  - 2. The angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection ($\angle i = \angle r$). [1.5 Marks]
- (c) **Lateral Inversion** [2 Marks]:
  - The phenomenon in which the left side of an object appears as the right side in its image formed by a plane mirror. Example: The word 'AMBULANCE' written in reverse on vehicles. [2 Marks]

### Question 6 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Distance vs Displacement** [3 Marks]:
  - Distance is actual path length; Displacement is shortest straight-line distance between initial and final points. [1 Mark]
  - Distance is scalar (magnitude only); Displacement is vector (magnitude and direction). [1 Mark]
  - Distance is always positive; Displacement can be positive, negative, or zero. [1 Mark]
- (b) **Acceleration Numerical** [4 Marks]:
  - $u = 0$, $v = 54\,\text{km/h} = 54 \times \frac{5}{18} = 15\,\text{m/s}$, $t = 10\,\text{s}$. [1 Mark]
  - Formula: $a = \frac{v - u}{t}$ [1 Mark]
  - Substitution: $a = \frac{15 - 0}{10} = 1.5\,\text{m/s}^2$. [2 Marks]
- (c) **Rolling Ball Motion** [3 Marks]:
  - Translatory (rectilinear) motion as it moves down the alley. [1.5 Marks]
  - Rotatory motion as it spins about its center of mass. [1.5 Marks]

### Question 7 Solutions [10 Marks]
- (a) **Conservation of Energy** [4 Marks]:
  - Statement: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, but only transformed from one form to another; the total energy of an isolated system remains constant. [2 Marks]
  - Example 1: Hydroelectric dam (Potential energy of water $\rightarrow$ Kinetic energy $\rightarrow$ Electrical energy). [1 Mark]
  - Example 2: Electric bulb (Electrical energy $\rightarrow$ Light and Heat energy). [1 Mark]
- (b) **Work Done Numerical** [3 Marks]:
  - $m = 50\,\text{kg}$, $g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$, $h = 4\,\text{m}$.
  - $F = mg = 50 \times 10 = 500\,\text{N}$. [1 Mark]
  - $W = F \times h = 500 \times 4 = 2000\,\text{J}$. [2 Marks]
- (c) **Renewable vs Non-Renewable** [3 Marks]:
  - Renewable sources replenish naturally and are inexhaustible (solar, wind, hydro). [1.5 Marks]
  - Non-renewable sources exist in finite amounts and deplete with use (coal, petroleum, natural gas). [1.5 Marks]
