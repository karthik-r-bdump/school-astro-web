## Section A: Multiple Choice Solutions (1 Mark Each)
1. **Question**: Value of $(-35) \times (-2) + (-35) \times 8$:
   - **Answer**: **B) $-210$** [1 Mark]
     - *Working*: $(-35) \times [(-2) + 8] = (-35) \times 6 = -210$.

   - **Answer**: **B) $18$** [1 Mark]

   - **Answer**: **B) $-1$** [1 Mark] (Odd power of $-1$ is always $-1$).

## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Evaluate $[(-13) + (-17)] \div [(-22) - (-20)]$:
   - Numerator: $-13 - 17 = -30$. [1 Mark]
   - Denominator: $-22 + 20 = -2$.
   - Division: $(-30) \div (-2) = 15$. [1 Mark]
2. **Question**: Verify commutative property:
   - LHS: $a + b = (-12) + 25 = 13$. [1 Mark]
   - RHS: $b + a = 25 + (-12) = 13$.
   - Since LHS = RHS, verified. [1 Mark]
3. **Question**: $(-25) \times 37 \times 4$:
   - Grouping: $[(-25) \times 4] \times 37 = (-100) \times 37 = -3700$. [2 Marks]
4. **Question**: Temperature problem:
   - Tuesday: $-5^\circ\text{C} - 2^\circ\text{C} = -7^\circ\text{C}$. [1 Mark]
   - Wednesday: $-7^\circ\text{C} + 4^\circ\text{C} = -3^\circ\text{C}$. [1 Mark]
5. **Question**: Divide $-156$ by $-12$:
   - $(-156) \div (-12) = +13$. [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. **Question**: Simplify $25 - [14 - \{18 - (12 - \overline{5 - 3})\}]$:
   - Step 1 (Vinculum): $5 - 3 = 2$. Inside round bracket: $12 - 2 = 10$. [1 Mark]
   - Step 2 (Curly bracket): $18 - 10 = 8$. [1 Mark]
   - Step 3 (Square bracket): $14 - 8 = 6$.
   - Final: $25 - 6 = 19$. [1 Mark]
