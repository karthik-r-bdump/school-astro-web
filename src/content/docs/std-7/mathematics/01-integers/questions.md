## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. The value of $(-35) \times (-2) + (-35) \times 8$ using the distributive property is:
   - A) $-350$
   - B) $-210$
   - C) $210$
   - D) $350$

2. The additive inverse of $-18$ is:
   - A) $-18$
   - B) $18$
   - C) $0$
   - D) $1$

3. $(-1)^{101}$ is equal to:
   - A) $1$
   - B) $-1$
   - C) $101$
   - D) $-101$

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. Evaluate: $[(-13) + (-17)] \div [(-22) - (-20)]$.
2. Verify the commutative property of addition for $a = -12$ and $b = 25$.
3. Find the product using convenient properties: $(-25) \times 37 \times 4$.
4. At Srinagar, temperature was $-5^\circ\text{C}$ on Monday and then it dropped by $2^\circ\text{C}$ on Tuesday. On Wednesday, it rose by $4^\circ\text{C}$. What was the temperature on Wednesday?
5. Divide $-156$ by $-12$.

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. Simplify using BODMAS:
   $$25 - [14 - \{18 - (12 - \overline{5 - 3})\}]$$ [3 Marks]

2. In a quiz competition:
   - Marks awarded for every correct answer: $+4$
   - Marks deducted for every incorrect answer: $-2$
   - (a) Radhika answered all 20 questions and scored 50 marks. How many questions did she answer correctly? [3 Marks]
   - (b) Rohit scored $-10$ marks though he got 5 correct answers. How many questions did he attempt incorrectly? [2 Marks]
