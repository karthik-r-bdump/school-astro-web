## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)
1. Identify the abstract noun in: "The soldier showed exceptional bravery in the battle."
   - A) Soldier
   - B) Battle
   - C) Bravery
   - D) Exceptional
2. "She baked the cake **herself**." The bold word is:
   - A) Reflexive pronoun
   - B) Emphatic pronoun
   - C) Demonstrative pronoun
   - D) Relative pronoun
3. The collective noun for a collection of ships is:
   - A) Herd
   - B) Fleet
   - C) Pack
   - D) Swarm

## Section B: Short Answer Questions (2 Marks Each)
1. Form abstract nouns from: (a) Honest, (b) Grow, (c) Hero, (d) Pure.
2. Differentiate between a reflexive pronoun and an emphatic pronoun with one example each.
3. Fill in the blanks with appropriate relative pronouns (*who, whom, whose, which, that*):
   - (a) The girl ______ mother is a doctor won the quiz.
   - (b) I have found the umbrella ______ I had lost yesterday.
4. Correct the pronoun errors:
   - (a) Each of the boys brought their own book.
   - (b) Between you and I, he is a cheat.
5. Give the possessive case form of: (a) The children / toys, (b) James / car.

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Questions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. Rewrite the sentences using pronouns as directed:
   - (a) Combine using a relative pronoun: "We met a traveler. He had walked across the desert." [2 Marks]
   - (b) Identify the case (Nominative, Accusative, or Possessive) of nouns: "The teacher gave Rohan a prize." [3 Marks]
2. Write a short paragraph (60–80 words) describing an act of kindness you witnessed, underlining at least two collective nouns and two abstract nouns. [5 Marks]
