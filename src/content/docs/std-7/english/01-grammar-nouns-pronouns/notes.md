# Chapter 1: Grammar: Nouns and Pronouns

## 🎯 Syllabus Scope & Objectives
- Nouns: Common, Proper, Collective, Abstract, Material.
- Pronouns: Personal, Possessive, Demonstrative, Interrogative, Relative, Reflexive, Emphatic.
- Subject-verb and pronoun-antecedent agreement.

---

## 📖 Key Concepts & Detailed Notes
### 1. Types of Nouns
- **Collective Nouns**: Group taken as a whole (e.g., *a pride of lions*, *a fleet of ships*, *a flock of sheep*).
- **Abstract Nouns**: Qualities, states, or concepts (e.g., *honesty, bravery, freedom, childhood*). Formed from adjectives (*brave* $\rightarrow$ *bravery*), verbs (*obey* $\rightarrow$ *obedience*), or common nouns (*child* $\rightarrow$ *childhood*).

### 2. Pronoun Categories
- **Reflexive**: Subject and object are same person ("He hurt **himself**").
- **Emphatic**: Used for emphasis ("The Principal **himself** distributed the prizes").
- **Relative**: Relates to an antecedent and connects clauses (*who, whom, whose, which, that*).
