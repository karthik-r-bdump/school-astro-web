## Section B: Short Answer Solutions (2 Marks Each)
1. (a) Honesty, (b) Growth, (c) Heroism, (d) Purity. [2 Marks]
2. Reflexive acts as direct/indirect object reflecting back on subject ("He hurt himself"); Emphatic adds emphasis and can be removed without grammatical loss ("I myself saw it"). [2 Marks]
3. (a) whose, (b) which / that. [2 Marks]
4. (a) Each of the boys brought **his** own book. (b) Between you and **me**, he is a cheat. [2 Marks]
5. (a) The children's toys, (b) James's car. [2 Marks]

## Section C: Structured & Long Answer Solutions (3–5 Marks Each)
1. (a) "We met a traveler **who** had walked across the desert." [2 Marks]
