## Question 1: Composition Marking Scheme [20 Marks]
- Content & Relevance: 8 Marks
- Vocabulary & Sentence Variety: 4 Marks
- Grammatical Accuracy & Tense Consistency: 4 Marks
- Cohesion, Organization & Paragraphing: 4 Marks

## Question 2: Letter Writing Marking Scheme [10 Marks]
- Format (Address, Date, Salutation, Subscription): 3 Marks
- Content & Development: 5 Marks
- Expression & Accuracy: 2 Marks

## Question 3: Notice & Email Format [10 Marks]
- Notice (Heading, Event, Date, Time, Venue, Target): 5 Marks
- Email (Recipient, Subject, Salutation, Body, Sign-off): 5 Marks

## Question 5: Applied Grammar Solutions [20 Marks]
- (a) Verbs: 1. had finished, 2. has been raining, 3. will leave, 4. knew, 5. was watching. [5 Marks]
- (b) Prepositions: 1. between, 2. into, 3. of, 4. of, 5. with. [5 Marks]
- (c) Joining sentences:
  1. *Though he was poor, he was completely honest.*
  2. *Hearing the loud noise, the baby woke up.*
  3. *Unless you work hard, you will not pass.*
  4. *As soon as the bell rang, the students ran out.* [5 Marks]
- (d) Transformations:
  1. *No other boy in the class is as tall as Rohan.*
  2. *The tiger was so fierce that it could not be tamed.*
  3. *He said that he was reading an interesting novel.*
  4. *A beautiful song was sung by the choir.* [5 Marks]
