# Class 7 English Language Midterm Specimen Examination

## Question 1: Composition [20 Marks]
Write a composition (250–300 words) on any ONE topic:
1. **Descriptive**: Describe a bustling railway station during the monsoon season.
2. **Narrative**: Write an original story beginning with: *"The thunderstorm had knocked out the electricity, and then we heard a strange knocking at the back door..."*
3. **Reflective**: "Failure is the stepping stone to success." Express your views with real-life examples.

## Question 2: Letter Writing [10 Marks]
Select any ONE:
1. **Formal Letter**: Write to the Municipal Commissioner complaining about frequent waterlogging and potholes in your locality.
2. **Informal Letter**: Write to your cousin inviting them to spend holidays with you in Bangalore.

## Question 3: Notice & Email Writing [10 Marks]
1. (a) Notice for an Inter-House Debate Competition. [5 Marks]
2. (b) Email to a neighboring school inviting their debate team. [5 Marks]

## Question 4: Reading Comprehension [20 Marks]
Answer comprehension questions on an unseen passage. [20 Marks]

## Question 5: Applied Grammar [20 Marks]
Fill in blanks with correct verb forms, prepositions, join sentences without 'and/but/so', and sentence transformations. [20 Marks]
