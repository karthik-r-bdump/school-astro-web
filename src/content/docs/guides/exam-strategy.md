# ICSE Study & Exam Strategy Guide

Homeschooling under the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) curriculum requires structured planning, continuous assessment, and thorough familiarity with board evaluation standards.

<Aside type="tip" title="Core ICSE Principle">
In CISCE examinations, keywords, scientific precision, step-by-step mathematical working, and proper units are essential for securing full marks.
</Aside>

---

## 📅 Effective Daily Study Routine

1. **Active Recall & Revision (First 20 Mins)**: Begin each study block by testing memory of previous concepts using flashcards or quick quiz questions.
2. **Core Concept Exploration (40 Mins)**: Study new chapter notes, analyze diagrams, and derive formulas.
3. **Unassisted Practice (30 Mins)**: Solve practice questions in `questions.md` without looking at solutions.
4. **Self-Correction & Analysis (20 Mins)**: Cross-check answers using `answers.md`, grade using the ICSE marking scheme, and note down missed keywords.

---

## 🎯 Subject-Wise High-Yield Strategies

### 1. Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- **Physics**: Always write the formula first [1 Mark], substitute values with appropriate SI units [1 Mark], and state the final numerical answer with units [1 Mark].
- **Chemistry**: Balanced chemical equations with state symbols ($s, l, g, aq$) and reaction conditions (temperature, catalyst) are mandatory.
- **Biology**: Diagrams must be neat, drawn in pencil, and labeled horizontally using ruler lines. Every definition must include the physiological function and anatomical location.

### 2. Mathematics
- Never skip intermediate calculation steps.
- In geometry, state the theorem or property used in brackets (e.g., *[Linear pair]* or *[Exterior angle theorem]*).

### 3. Languages
- Practice reading comprehension by writing answers in your own words rather than copying whole sentences verbatim from the passage.
- Follow standard ICSE letter formats (formal and informal) precisely.
