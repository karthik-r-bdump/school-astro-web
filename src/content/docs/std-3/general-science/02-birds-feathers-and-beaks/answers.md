## Section B: Short Answer Solutions
1. **Question**: Why hollow bones:
   - **Answer**: Hollow bones are light in weight, making it easier for birds to lift off and fly in the air.
2. **Question**: Three types of feathers:
   - Down feathers: Keep the body warm.
   - Flight feathers: Help in flapping wings and steering flight.
   - Body feathers: Cover the body and give it a streamlined shape.
3. **Question**: Hummingbird beak:
   - **Answer**: To reach deep into flowers and suck sweet nectar like a drinking straw.
4. **Question**: Duck's webbed feet:
   - **Answer**: Skin between toes acts like paddles/oars to push water backwards while swimming.
5. **Question**: Eagle vs Sparrow beak:
   - Eagle has a sharp, hooked beak for tearing meat; Sparrow has a short, hard, pointed beak for cracking seeds.

## Section C: Problem Solving Solutions
1. **Question**: Bird adaptations:
   - (a) Woodpecker: Uses its chisel-like beak to tap wood, drill into tree trunks, and catch insects hiding under bark.
   - (b) Birds of prey: Hooked beaks allow them to tear meat and flesh from their prey easily.
   - (c) Perching birds: Sparrows and crows have 3 toes in front and 1 behind that curl around branches to lock tightly in place.
