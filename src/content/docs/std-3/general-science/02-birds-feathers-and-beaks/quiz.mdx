<Quiz title="Beaks of Birds of Prey" autoNumber>
What kind of beak does an eagle or a hawk have?

- [x] Strong, sharp, and hooked beak for tearing flesh
- [ ] Broad and flat beak with strainers
- [ ] Short, hard, and pointed beak for crushing seeds
- [ ] Long, slender, curved beak for sucking nectar

**Explanation:** Birds of prey like eagles, hawks, and owls have strong, hooked beaks that allow them to catch prey and tear meat easily.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Warmth Feathers" autoNumber>
Which type of feathers are soft, fluffy, and stay close to a bird's skin to keep its body warm?

- [ ] Flight feathers
- [x] Down feathers
- [ ] Body feathers
- [ ] Tail feathers

**Explanation:** Down feathers are short and fluffy feathers located right next to the bird's skin. They trap air to keep the bird warm in chilly weather.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Swimming Birds" autoNumber>
What special feature helps ducks and swans swim smoothly through water?

- [ ] Sharp claws (talons)
- [x] Webbed feet with skin between their toes
- [ ] Two toes pointing forward and two backward
- [ ] Long legs with spread-out toes for wading

**Explanation:** Ducks have webbed feet where flaps of skin join the three front toes, acting just like paddles or flippers to push through water!
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Flightless Birds" autoNumber>
Which of the following are birds that cannot fly? (Select all that apply)

- [x] Ostrich
- [x] Penguin
- [x] Kiwi
- [ ] Sparrow
- [ ] Pigeon

**Explanation:** Ostriches, penguins, and kiwis are flightless birds. Ostriches run fast with strong legs, while penguins use their wings as flippers for swimming.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Steering in Flight" autoNumber>
The large, strong feathers on a bird's wings and tail that help it fly and change direction are [[flight]] feathers.

**Explanation:** Flight feathers are long and sturdy. Wing flight feathers provide lift, and tail flight feathers act like a rudder to steer and balance while flying.
</Quiz>

<Quiz title="Chisel Beaks" autoNumber>
Which bird uses its chisel-shaped beak to drill holes into tree trunks to catch insects?

- [ ] Hummingbird
- [x] Woodpecker
- [ ] Pelican
- [ ] Parrot

**Explanation:** Woodpeckers have sharp, pointed, chisel-like beaks and strong neck muscles to drill deep into wood to pull out hidden bugs.
</Quiz>
