# Chapter 2: Birds – Feathers, Beaks, and Claws

## 🎯 What We Will Learn
- Features of birds: Feathers, hollow bones, wings, beaks (no teeth).
- Types of feathers: Flight feathers, Down feathers, Body feathers.
- Different types of beaks and their functions:
  - Short, hard, pointed beaks for seed crushing (Sparrow, Pigeon).
  - Strong, curved/hooked beaks for tearing flesh (Eagle, Hawk, Vulture).
  - Chisel-shaped beaks for drilling wood (Woodpecker).
  - Broad, flat beaks with strainers for water feeding (Duck).
  - Long, slender beaks for sipping nectar (Sunbird, Hummingbird).
- Feet and claws: Perching birds, climbing birds, swimming birds (webbed feet), birds of prey (talons).

---

## 📖 Chapter Notes & Simple Explanations
### 1. Unique Characteristics of Birds
- Birds are the only animals with **feathers**.
- They have **hollow bones** filled with air, which make their bodies lightweight for flight.
- Instead of teeth, birds have **beaks** suited to their diet.

### 2. Types of Feathers
- **Down Feathers**: Soft, fluffy feathers located close to the skin; keep the bird's body warm.
- **Flight Feathers**: Long, strong feathers on the wings and tail; assist in flying and changing flight direction.
- **Body Feathers**: Cover the outer body and give the bird its streamlined shape.

### 3. Types of Beaks
| Bird | Beak Type | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sparrow, Pigeon** | Short, hard, conical | Crushing hard seeds and grains. |
| **Eagle, Hawk, Owl** | Strong, hooked, sharp | Tearing flesh of small prey. |
| **Woodpecker** | Long, chisel-shaped | Drilling holes into tree bark to pull out insects. |
| **Duck** | Broad, flat with holes (strainers) on sides | Scoops muddy water; mud and water drain out through holes, leaving insects and plants inside. |
| **Sunbird, Hummingbird** | Long, thin, curved straw-like | Sucking sweet nectar from flower blossoms. |

### 4. Claws and Feet
- **Webbed Feet (Duck)**: Skin connects the front toes like flippers, helping them paddle in water.
- **Talons (Eagle, Hawk)**: Strong, curved sharp claws for catching and holding live prey.
- **Perching Birds (Sparrow, Crow)**: Three toes in front, one behind; enables them to grip tree branches tightly even while sleeping.
