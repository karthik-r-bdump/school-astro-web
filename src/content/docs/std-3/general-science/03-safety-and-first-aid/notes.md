# Chapter 3: Safety and First Aid

## 🎯 What We Will Learn
- Importance of safety rules at home, in school, on the road, and in the playground.
- Home Safety: Not playing with fire, sharp objects (knives, scissors), or electrical appliances with wet hands.
- School Safety: No running on stairs, no pushing on corridors.
- Road Safety: Using zebra crossings, walking on footpaths, traffic light rules (Red, Yellow, Green).
- First Aid: Immediate help given to an injured person before the doctor arrives.
- First aid for minor cuts, burns, insect stings, and nosebleeds.

---

## 📖 Chapter Notes & Simple Explanations
### 1. Safety Rules
- **At Home**:
  - Never touch electrical switches or appliances with wet hands (risk of electric shock).
  - Do not play with sharp objects like knives, scissors, blades, or matchsticks.
  - Do not run on wet bathroom floors; keep toys cleared from staircases to prevent tripping.
- **On the Road**:
  - Always walk on the **footpath** / pavement.
  - Cross the road only at the **Zebra Crossing** after looking left, right, and left again.
  - Follow traffic signals:
    - **Red**: STOP
    - **Yellow**: GET READY
    - **Green**: GO
  - Never lean out of a moving bus or get on/off a running vehicle.
- **In School**:
  - Do not run on staircases or slide down banisters.
  - Do not push each other while standing in line.

### 2. First Aid Basics
- **First Aid**: The first medical assistance provided to an injured or sick person before professional medical help arrives.
- **First Aid Kit Contents**: Band-aids, sterile gauze bandages, cotton, antiseptic liquid (Dettol/Savlon), burn ointment, scissors, adhesive tape.
- **Handling Emergencies**:
  - **Minor Cut/Scrape**: Wash with clean water, apply antiseptic cream, and cover with a clean band-aid.
  - **Minor Burn**: Immediately place under cold running water for 10–15 minutes. Never apply ice directly. Apply antiseptic burn ointment.
  - **Nosebleed**: Sit upright, lean slightly forward (never backward), and pinch the soft part of the nose for 5–10 minutes. Place an ice pack on the forehead.
