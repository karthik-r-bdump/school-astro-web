## Section B: Short Answer Solutions
1. **Question**: Define First Aid:
   - **Answer**: The immediate help or medical care given to an injured person before a doctor or ambulance arrives.
2. **Question**: Two home safety rules:
   - Never play with sharp objects like knives or blades.
   - Never touch electrical sockets or switches with wet hands.
3. **Question**: Wet hands and switches:
   - **Answer**: Water is a good conductor of electricity, which can cause a severe or fatal electric shock.
4. **Question**: Nosebleed first aid:
   - **Answer**: Sit up, lean slightly forward, pinch the soft part of the nose for 5–10 minutes, and place an ice pack on the forehead.
5. **Question**: Four items in first aid kit:
   - **Answer**: Band-aids, antiseptic cream (Savlon/Dettol), sterile cotton/gauze, and scissors.

## Section C: Problem Solving Solutions
1. **Question**: Safety in school and road:
   - (a) Red means Stop, Yellow means Get Ready, Green means Go.
   - (b) Do not push on swings/slides; never throw sand or stones at others; wait for your turn.
   - (c) Leaning backward causes blood to flow down the throat and stomach, causing nausea or choking; leaning forward lets blood drain out safely.
