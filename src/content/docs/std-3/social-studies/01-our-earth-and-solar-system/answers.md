## Section A: Objective Solutions
1. **Question**: Largest planet:
   - **Answer**: **C) Jupiter**
2. **Question**: Earth's rotation causes:
   - **Answer**: **B) Day and Night**
3. **Question**: Largest ocean:
   - **Answer**: **C) Pacific Ocean**

---

## Section B: Short Answer Solutions
1. **Question**: Blue Planet:
   - **Answer**: Because nearly three-fourths (71%) of its surface is covered with water (oceans and seas), appearing blue from space.
2. **Question**: 8 Planets in order:
   - **Answer**: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
3. **Question**: 7 Continents:
   - **Answer**: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia.
4. **Question**: Rotation vs Revolution:
   - Rotation is spinning on its axis (24 hours, causes day and night); Revolution is orbiting the Sun (365 1/4 days, causes seasons).
5. **Question**: Revolution time:
   - **Answer**: 365 days and 6 hours (or 365 1/4 days).

## Section C: Problem Solving Solutions
1. **Question**: Earth unique features:
   - (a) Moderate temperature suitable for life, presence of liquid water, and breathable atmosphere rich in oxygen.
   - (b) Largest continent: **Asia**; Smallest continent: **Australia**.
   - (c) **Antarctica** is called the White Continent because it is permanently covered with thick sheets of snow and ice.
