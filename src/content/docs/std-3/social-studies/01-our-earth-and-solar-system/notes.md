# Chapter 1: Our Earth and the Solar System

## 🎯 What We Will Learn
- The Sun and the 8 planets in order (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune).
- Earth: The unique planet (presence of water, oxygen atmosphere, suitable temperature).
- Shape of the Earth: Spherical (geoid), proof from Magellan's voyage and horizon view.
- Continents (7) and Oceans (5).
- Rotation (causes Day & Night) and Revolution (causes Seasons).

---

## 📖 Chapter Notes & Simple Explanations
### 1. The Solar System
- The Sun is a giant star at the center of our solar system providing heat and light.
- **8 Planets in order from the Sun**:
  1. Mercury (closest, smallest)
  2. Venus (hottest, brightest, morning/evening star)
  3. Earth (our home, the blue planet)
  4. Mars (the red planet)
  5. Jupiter (largest planet)
  6. Saturn (planet with beautiful rings)
  7. Uranus (cold gas giant)
  8. Neptune (farthest planet)

### 2. Earth: The Blue Planet
- 71% of the Earth's surface is covered with water; 29% is land.
- **7 Continents**: Asia (largest), Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica (frozen), Europe, Australia (smallest/island continent).
- **5 Oceans**: Pacific (largest and deepest), Atlantic, Indian, Southern (Antarctic), Arctic (smallest).

### 3. Movements of the Earth
- **Rotation**: Spinning of Earth on its own tilted axis from West to East. Takes **24 hours** (1 day); causes **Day and Night**.
- **Revolution**: Movement of Earth around the Sun in a fixed elliptical orbit. Takes **365 days and 6 hours** (1 year); causes **Seasons**.
