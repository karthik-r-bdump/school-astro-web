## Worksheet 1: Numbers, Addition & Subtraction [25 Marks]
1. Write in figures: Six thousand four hundred twenty-nine. [1 Mark]
2. Add: $3482 + 2179$. [2 Marks]
3. Subtract: $7000 - 3458$. [2 Marks]
4. Solve the word problem: A farmer harvested $1450\,\text{kg}$ of rice in 2024 and $2185\,\text{kg}$ in 2025. How much rice did he harvest in total? [3 Marks]
5. Find the missing digit:
   $$\begin{array}{r} 4 \; \Box \; 6 \; 2 \\ + 2 \; 3 \; 1 \; 5 \\ \hline 6 \; 8 \; 7 \; 7 \end{array}$$ [2 Marks]

## Worksheet 2: Multiplication & Division [25 Marks]
1. $24 \times 6 = \text{______}$ [1 Mark]
2. $108 \div 9 = \text{______}$ [1 Mark]
3. Multiply $345 \times 4$. [2 Marks]
4. Divide $84 \div 4$ and write the Quotient and Remainder. [2 Marks]
5. A box contains 8 packets of crayons. Each packet has 12 crayons. How many crayons are there in total? [3 Marks]

## Worksheet 3: Rounding & Mental Math [25 Marks]
1. Round $58$ to the nearest 10. [1 Mark]
2. Round $472$ to the nearest 100. [1 Mark]
3. What number must be added to $450$ to make $1000$? [2 Marks]
4. How many tens are there in $340$? [1 Mark]
5. If 1 notebook costs Rs 25, find the cost of 4 notebooks. [2 Marks]
