## Section B: Short Questions
1. Round each of the following to the nearest 10:
   - (a) $64$
   - (b) $98$
   - (c) $125$
   - (d) $304$
2. Round each of the following to the nearest 100:
   - (a) $450$
   - (b) $819$
   - (c) $1462$
   - (d) $2738$
3. A fruit seller has $76$ apples. Round off the number of apples to the nearest 10.
4. Rahul bought a toy for Rs $348$. To the nearest hundred, how much money did Rahul spend?
5. Write all the two-digit numbers that round to $50$ when rounded to the nearest 10.

## Section C: Problem Solving / Descriptive
1. Estimation Word Problems:
   - (a) A baker baked $284$ cookies in the morning and $318$ cookies in the afternoon. Estimate the total number of cookies baked to the nearest hundred.
   - (b) A school bus travels $47\,\text{km}$ on Monday and $53\,\text{km}$ on Tuesday. Round each distance to the nearest 10 and find the estimated total distance.
