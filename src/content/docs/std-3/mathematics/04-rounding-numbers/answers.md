## Section A: Objective Solutions
1. **Question**: Round $47$ to nearest 10:
   - **Answer**: **50**
2. **Question**: Round $82$ to nearest 10:
   - **Answer**: **80**
3. **Question**: Round $35$ to nearest 10:
   - **Answer**: **40** (5 rounds up)
4. **Question**: Round $238$ to nearest 100:
   - **Answer**: **200** (tens digit 3 is $< 5$)
5. **Question**: Round $675$ to nearest 100:
   - **Answer**: **700** (tens digit 7 is $\ge 5$)

## Section C: Problem Solving Solutions
1. **Question**: Estimation word problems:
   - (a) $284$ rounds to $300$; $318$ rounds to $300$.
     - Estimated total $= 300 + 300 = \mathbf{600\text{ cookies}}$.
   - (b) $47\,\text{km} \rightarrow 50\,\text{km}$; $53\,\text{km} \rightarrow 50\,\text{km}$.
     - Estimated total $= 50 + 50 = \mathbf{100\,\text{km}}$.
