# Chapter 1: Numbers and Place Value

## 🎯 What We Will Learn
- Reading and writing 4-digit numbers (up to 9999).
- Place value and Face value of digits in Thousands, Hundreds, Tens, and Ones.
- Expanded form and Standard form.
- Comparing and ordering numbers ($<, >, =$, ascending, descending).
- Successor ($+1$) and Predecessor ($-1$).
- Forming largest and smallest numbers using given digits.

---

## 📖 Chapter Notes & Simple Explanations
### 1. 4-Digit Numbers and Place Value
- The smallest 4-digit number is **1000** (One Thousand).
- The largest 4-digit number is **9999** (Nine Thousand Nine Hundred Ninety-Nine).
- **Place Value vs Face Value**:
  - In the number **4,825**:
    - **Face value** is the digit itself: Face value of 8 is 8.
    - **Place value** is the value of the digit based on its position:
      - 4 is in Thousands place $\rightarrow 4 \times 1000 = 4000$.
      - 8 is in Hundreds place $\rightarrow 8 \times 100 = 800$.
      - 2 is in Tens place $\rightarrow 2 \times 10 = 20$.
      - 5 is in Ones place $\rightarrow 5 \times 1 = 5$.

### 2. Expanded Form
- Standard Form: $4825$
- Expanded Form: $4000 + 800 + 20 + 5$

### 3. Comparing and Forming Numbers
- To compare two 4-digit numbers: Compare thousands digit first; if equal, compare hundreds digit, then tens, then ones.
  - Example: $5340 > 5299$.
- **Forming Numbers**:
  - Given digits: $7, 2, 9, 4$
  - Largest number (arrange descending): **9742**
  - Smallest number (arrange ascending): **2479** (Note: 0 cannot be the first digit!).
