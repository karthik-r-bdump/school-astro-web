# Chapter 1: Spelling and Vocabulary

## 🎯 What We Will Learn
- Grade 3 high-frequency spelling words: modern, processed, concern, outsourcing, diversity, ceiling, apartments, strength, excitement, accepting, patient, comfortable, passive, static, economic.
- Sight words and silent letters (e.g. *k* in knight, *b* in doubt, *w* in write).
- Word meanings, synonyms, and antonyms.
- Building compound words (e.g. sun + flower = sunflower).
- Using words in meaningful sentences.

---

## 📖 Chapter Notes & Simple Explanations
### 1. High-Frequency Vocabulary List
Here are important Grade 3 words with their meanings and usage:

1. **Modern**: Belonging to the present or recent times.
   - *Sentence*: Our city has many **modern** buildings.
2. **Concern**: Worry or care about someone or something.
   - *Sentence*: The doctor showed deep **concern** for the sick child.
3. **Diversity**: A range of different things or people; variety.
   - *Sentence*: India is known for its cultural **diversity**.
4. **Ceiling**: The upper interior surface of a room.
   - *Spelling Rule*: Remember the rule — *'i' before 'e' except after 'c'* (**c-e-i-l-i-n-g**)!
5. **Strength**: The quality of being strong; physical power.
   - *Sentence*: Regular exercise builds muscle **strength**.
6. **Excitement**: A feeling of great enthusiasm and eagerness.
   - *Sentence*: The children shouted with **excitement** when the vacation began.
7. **Patient**: Able to wait calmly without getting annoyed.
   - *Sentence*: The teacher was very **patient** while answering our questions.
8. **Comfortable**: Providing physical ease and relaxation.
   - *Sentence*: I sat on the soft and **comfortable** sofa.

### 2. Silent Letters Guide
- **Silent 'k'**: *knee, knife, knight, know, knock*.
- **Silent 'w'**: *write, wrong, wrist, wrap, sword*.
- **Silent 'b'**: *climb, thumb, comb, lamb, doubt*.
